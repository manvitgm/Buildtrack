About Project

BuildTrack is a Construction Material Management System developed to manage and monitor construction materials efficiently.


This project provides an easy way to handle supplier details, inventory management, material orders, deliveries, payments, reviews, and notifications through a centralized dashboard.


The system helps construction companies reduce manual work and improve material tracking for different project sites.


Users can monitor stock availability, manage supplier information, track deliveries, and maintain payment records easily.
